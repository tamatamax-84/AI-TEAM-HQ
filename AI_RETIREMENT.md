# AI RETIREMENT / RECOVERY

## Lifecycle

`NOT_CONNECTED` → `ACTIVE` → `LIMITED` → `SUSPENDED` / `UNAVAILABLE` → `ACTIVE`

## Rules
- An AI may be temporarily rested to conserve credits or avoid unnecessary use.
- Credit exhaustion should not be treated as a permanent failure.
- A suspended AI receives no new work until its availability is verified.
- Non-Chief work may be reassigned during suspension when practical.
- High-load work is not reassigned away from ChatGPT / Chief under the default policy.
- When an AI returns, verify availability before restoring `ACTIVE` status.
- Record meaningful status changes when they affect task routing.
